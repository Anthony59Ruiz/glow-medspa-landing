Good to know

Questions, answered

Everything you might be wondering, before you ever walk in.

Do the treatments hurt, or need downtime?+

Most are comfortable and fit easily into your day. Botox is a few quick pinches with no downtime; facials are pure relaxation; and our laser treatments use numbing and cooling so you can head straight back to your routine. We'll walk you through exactly what to expect beforehand.

How do I know which treatment is right for me?+

That's exactly what your complimentary consultation is for. We listen first, look at your skin and your goals, and build a plan tailored to you — never a one-size-fits-all menu. Sometimes that means doing less than you expected.

When will I see results, and how long do they last?+

It depends on the treatment. Botox settles over three to seven days and lasts around three to four months; facials give an immediate glow that deepens with regular visits; and laser results build gradually over a series. We'll set clear, honest expectations so you're never guessing.

Is it safe? Who performs the treatments?+

Every treatment is performed by trained, licensed professionals in a private, meticulously clean studio. Your safety and comfort always come first — and we'll never recommend something that isn't right for you.

What does it cost, and do you offer consultations?+

Your first consultation is complimentary and completely no-pressure. Pricing depends on your personalized plan, and we'll walk you through everything transparently before you commit to anything. No surprises, ever.
